How long has the company existed?

Business programmes are for registered companies. Join the list anyway: we will tell you when it makes sense to register a company and what options open up then.

Company size

With 250 or more employees the company is not a small or medium-sized enterprise, and most business programmes are for SMEs. You can continue: our partner will check whether there is a suitable programme for large enterprises.

Questions

How much does the check cost?+

Nothing. The check and joining the waiting list are free and put you under no obligation.

Is funding guaranteed?+

No. Joining the list is not a guarantee. Programmes open at different times, have their own conditions and ranking, and not every idea or project can be funded.

How long does it take?+

From idea to approval usually takes between 1 and 2 years. That is why the waiting list is for future plans, not for projects you need right away.

Why do you need the company ID?+

So that our partner can check the company’s registration and public financial statements in the Commercial Register before the call. That way the conversation is specific rather than general.

Who will see my data?+

We and our partner Eco Globe Ltd., which does the check and prepares the application. We do not share it with anyone else and do not use it for advertising.

Can I leave the list?+

Yes, at any time by emailing us. We delete your data from the list.
