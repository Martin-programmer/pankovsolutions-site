The task

Domoupravitel BG manages residential buildings: it collects fees, organises owners’ meetings, takes reports from residents and works with lift and cleaning companies. Much of this lived in spreadsheets, emails and staff phones.

They needed one place for all buildings, owners, contracts and reports, and a way for new team members to get up to speed quickly without someone training them from scratch every time.

  • Data for every building and owner
  • Client journey from enquiry to contract
  • Reports with a clear status
  • Contract reminders
  • Roles and permissions
  • Online courses with quizzes
  • Certificates
  • Works on the phone

A CRM that speaks the building manager’s language

We started from Odoo and reconfigured it for residential buildings. We added our own tab with building data, custom stages for sales and for reports, and automations for the things most easily missed.

Buildings and owners

Each building has its own tab with flats, garages, parking spaces, commercial units, the lift, the monthly fee and how costs are split. For owners, the flat, floor and ownership share are kept.

Contracts under control

The signing date, term and expiry date are on the building card. Service companies for the lift, cleaning and access fobs are linked to it.

The new client’s journey

Enquiries move through clear stages: new enquiry, meeting and building inspection, quote sent, owners’ meeting and signed contract.

Reports and complaints

Every report from a resident has its own path: new, in progress, waiting for an external company, resolved and closed. Nothing stays “on someone’s phone”.

Teams and roles

Separate teams for sales and for service after the contract. Administrators, managers and staff see and change only what they need.

Tags and filters

Contacts are split into buildings, owners, tenants, suppliers and institutions, tagged by status, building type and district.

Reminders come by themselves

Four automatic rules run in the background. Nobody has to remember which contract is expiring or which report is pending; the system tells the right person.

Enquiry assignment

Every 30 minutes new enquiries are automatically assigned to a staff member.

Priority for referrals

An enquiry that comes from a referral automatically gets a higher priority.

Overdue reports

Every day the system sends reminders about reports not resolved on time.

Expiring contracts

Every day the system sends reminders about contracts expiring in the next 30 days.

An online academy for the team

The second system is a Moodle learning platform with the company’s logo and colours, fully in Bulgarian. A new employee logs in, works through the courses at their own pace, takes a quiz and gets a certificate.

  • Courses by category with video, documents and SCORM content
  • Quizzes with a question bank and automatic grading
  • Progress tracking and a certificate on completion
  • A calendar with deadlines and reports for management
  • Roles: administrator, trainer and learner

The biggest challenges

A standard CRM for a non-standard business

Off-the-shelf CRMs think in deals and products, while a building manager thinks in buildings, flats and ownership shares. We added custom fields and a tab so everything important about a building is on one screen.

Not losing reports

A broken lift or a leak in the basement can’t wait. Every report has an owner and a status, and overdue ones come up as a reminder every day until they are resolved.

Not letting contracts expire unnoticed

An expiring contract is a lost client if nobody notices in time. The system warns 30 days ahead, so there is time for a conversation and a new owners’ meeting.

A new employee, ready to work

Training every new person took up experienced colleagues’ time. Now the basics, the law and working with the CRM are in courses with quizzes, and management can see who has got how far.
