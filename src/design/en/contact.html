Before you write to us

How much does the consultation cost?+

Nothing. The first conversation is free and puts you under no obligation. The goal is to understand the task and tell you honestly what makes sense.

Do you work with companies from all over Bulgaria?+

Yes. Most meetings, training and support are online. When the task requires it, for example a network, a server or a warehouse, we come on site.

I’m a client and have a problem with my system. Where do I write?+

In the support portal, so the request gets a number and you can see its status. For urgent problems, call +359 877 944 224.

What should I write in the enquiry?+

A few sentences about what is getting in your way today or what you want to achieve. We will work out the details together.
