What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They let the website remember your choice, work securely and, if you allow it, measure visits and advertising. In this policy, “cookies” also covers similar technologies such as pixels and local browser storage.

This policy supplements our Privacy policy, which describes how we process personal data in general.

How we ask for consent

On your first visit we show a banner in which you can, equally easily:

  • reject all cookies that are not strictly necessary;
  • accept all;
  • open Settings and choose analytics and marketing cookies separately.

The “Reject all” and “Accept all” buttons are the same size and style. No categories are pre-ticked. The website works fully even if you refuse.

Before you make a choice, no Google or Meta tool is loaded and no analytics or marketing cookies are set. Closing the page or continuing to browse is not considered consent.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 4a of the Bulgarian Electronic Communications Act and Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent.

Types of cookies

Strictly necessary

Always active

The website cannot work properly without them: they remember your cookie choice and protect the forms from spam. They don’t require consent because they are strictly necessary for the service you have requested.

Analytics

Only with consent

Google Analytics: how many people visit the website, which pages they view and where they come from. They help us improve the website.

Marketing

Only with consent

Google Ads and Meta Pixel: they measure enquiries from our ads and let us show our ads on Google, Facebook and Instagram to people who have visited the website.

Google Consent Mode and Meta

We use Google Consent Mode v2 in its basic implementation: by default all signals (analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data, ad_personalization) are denied, and Google tags are not loaded at all until you give consent. For the Meta pixel, consent is passed through Meta’s consent mechanism, and the pixel is loaded only if you allow marketing cookies.

Google Ireland Limited and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited may transfer data to the USA on the basis of the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses.

How to change your choice

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page or the button here.

Cookie settings

When you withdraw consent, we delete the analytics and marketing cookies from our domain and stop the tools. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge.

Recording consent

So that we can prove we have received consent (Art. 7(1) GDPR), we keep a record of every choice: an anonymous consent identifier, the date and time, the version of this policy, the chosen categories and how the choice was made (accept, reject or custom settings). The record contains no name, email or IP address.

The record is kept in the ps_consent cookie in your browser and on our server for up to 3 years after the last change of your choice.

How long we keep your choice

We keep your choice, whether consent or refusal, for 12 months. After that, or if we change this policy significantly, the banner is shown again so you can confirm or change your choice.
