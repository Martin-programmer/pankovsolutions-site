Guest app with a loyalty programme and a wheel of fortune

We built an app from scratch that turns every visit to Secret Bar into part of a game: you collect hookahs and drinks, spin the wheel and win rewards. Behind the scenes, the bar runs everything from its own admin panel.

The task

Secret Bar wanted guests to come back more often and to have a reason to choose them. Paper loyalty cards get lost, are easy to fake and tell the bar nothing about its guests.

The task was an app with no app-store install that opens by scanning the QR code on the table. It had to be fun for guests, easy for staff on a busy night and fully manageable by the bar, without every change going through a developer.

For guests

The guest scans the QR code, signs up with email or Google, and can add the app to their phone’s home screen like a regular app.

Loyalty programme

Every hookah and every drink is recorded on the phone. The hookah is “assembled” piece by piece, and the glasses fill up until the guest reaches a free reward.

Wheel of fortune

Once every 24 hours the guest spins the wheel. The discount won goes to “Available discounts”, is shown to a staff member and stays in the history as used, with a date.

VIP and personal discounts

The bar marks regular guests as VIP and gives them a personal discount. The guest gets an email the moment this happens.

Menu with mixes

Hookahs and drinks come with a description, strength and amount of smoke, filters by strength and favourites. The guest chooses and shows the choice to the staff.

Service rating

After a visit, the guest rates the staff member who served them. The ratings go to the manager, not public.

Events and promotions

Upcoming nights and daily promotions are in the app, and booking is one click away.

For staff

Visits and rewards are confirmed by a staff member to prevent abuse. The staff member enters a short code, and there is a tablet at the bar that scans the QR code on the guest’s phone.

  • QR codes are single-use and short-lived
  • The tablet is locked with a separate PIN
  • A used discount stays in the history with a date
  • A deactivated guest loses access without their data being deleted

An admin panel with nothing hard-coded

Every value in the app is changed by the bar: rewards, wheel odds, hours until the next spin, texts, emails and rules. A developer is only needed for new features.

The biggest challenges

Rewards that can’t be faked

Every visit, reward and discount goes through confirmation by a staff member with a code or a single-use QR. That way the loyalty programme has value for both the bar and its guests.

An app without an app store

Guests don’t want to download yet another app for one bar. We built a web app that opens from the QR code and can be added to the home screen like a real one, with fast loading on phones.

The bar’s atmosphere on the phone

A dark design with gold, a custom illustration of a hookah that assembles itself, and a wheel with a real game feel. The app had to look like part of the venue, not a template.

Putting the bar fully in control

Dozens of settings that usually live in the code were moved into the admin panel. The bar launches promotions, changes rewards and writes the emails itself, without waiting for anyone.
