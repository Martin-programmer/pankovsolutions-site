What an ERP system is

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) is software that brings a company’s core processes into one shared database: sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing, invoicing and people.

Instead of each department keeping its own spreadsheet, everyone works on the same data. When a salesperson confirms an order, the warehouse sees it immediately and the invoice is created from it, without anyone re-typing anything.

Without ERP

  • Orders in emails and Viber
  • Stock levels in Excel
  • Invoices in a separate program
  • Prices in a notebook
  • Reports assembled by hand
  • “Ask Ivan, he knows”

How it works in practice

Here is the path of an order. In an ERP system it is one and the same record moving through different departments, not seven separate documents.

  1. QuoteThe salesperson sends a quote from the system.
  2. OrderThe customer confirms; the quote becomes an order.
  3. InventoryGoods are reserved or sent to production.
  4. DeliveryThe warehouse sees what to ship and to whom.
  5. InvoiceThe invoice is created from the order.
  6. PaymentThe payment is recorded and the receivable closed.
  7. ReportManagement sees everything in real time.

It’s time for an ERP if

  • The same data is entered in several places
  • You don’t know the exact stock without going to the warehouse
  • Monthly reports take days
  • Important information is in the heads of one or two people
  • The company is growing and Excel is no longer enough
  • You use several programs that don’t talk to each other

Maybe not yet, if

  • A one- or two-person company with a few documents a month
  • You are only looking for accounting software
  • You want a system that never changes

How much does an ERP system cost?+

It depends on the number of modules, the add-ons and the volume of data to migrate. After the free consultation you receive a quote with a specific scope, timeline and fixed price. Since Odoo Community is open source, there is no per-user licence, and after launch you pay only for the server and, if you wish, support.

Why Odoo Community and not Enterprise?+

Enterprise is the paid version of Odoo, with a monthly fee for every user. For most small and medium-sized companies, Community, extended with OCA community modules and our add-ons, covers their needs without the ongoing licence cost.

Can accounting be done in Odoo?+

Odoo issues invoices and tracks payments. Most companies in Bulgaria keep their books with their accountant, in the software the accountant uses. We make sure the data reaches them without re-typing.

How long does implementation take?+

A typical project is ready in about 45 days. A smaller scope, for example sales and inventory, can be quicker, and large projects are split into phases.

Can we start with only some of the modules?+

Yes, and that is often the best option. You start with what hurts most, and we add the other modules once the team is used to the system.

What happens to our data in Excel and the old program?+

We transfer customers, products, prices and stock before launch. We check them together with you so you start with clean data.

Does it work on a phone?+

Yes. Odoo works in the browser and on phones, so salespeople and the warehouse have access on the go.

Where is the data stored?+

On a server of your choice, in the cloud or in the office. We also set up automatic backups.
