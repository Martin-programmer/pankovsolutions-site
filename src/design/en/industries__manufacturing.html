The journey of one order

This is what it looks like when the system covers the whole journey. You can start with just part of it, for example enquiries and quotes.

  1. EnquiryArrives with a drawing or model and is logged at once.
  2. QuotePrepared in the system and signed online by the customer.
  3. AdvanceThe payment is recorded against the order.
  4. MaterialsYou see what is in stock and what to order.
  5. ProductionEveryone knows what is next and when it is due.
  6. InvoiceGoes to the accounting software with no retyping.

Real project

Ravera, metalworking, Aytos

Ravera makes parts and press moulds to customer drawings. Enquiries, quotes and drawings lived in email and on separate computers, with no shared view of sales. Drawings, 3D models and machine programs are the company's most valuable asset, and some of them sat on an external disk.

We built three connected systems: a CRM where every enquiry moves from drawing to order, office network protection with a single sign-on, and automatic backups that ransomware cannot delete. Invoices stay in Microinvest, and the team was trained on every system.

What manufacturing companies ask

Will it work with our accounting software?+

In most cases, yes. At Ravera invoices stay in Microinvest with no retyping. We check the connection for your software in the plan, before we start.

Our shop floor staff do not use computers. Will they manage?+

Shop-floor screens are simple and open on a phone or tablet. We train everyone on their own part, without extra fields and menus.

Will we have to stop work during the rollout?+

No. We launch the system in stages. While we configure and test, the company works as before.

Will our drawings and machine programs be safe?+

Yes. If needed, we add automatic backups with a copy in another data centre that ransomware cannot delete, as we did for Ravera.
