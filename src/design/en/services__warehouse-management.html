What a warehouse system is

A WMS (Warehouse Management System) is software that tracks every movement of goods: from receiving from the supplier, through the place on the shelf, to shipping to the customer.

Every item has an exact location, every movement is scanned and stock is updated instantly. So sales don’t promise goods you don’t have, and the warehouse picks instead of searching.

Today

  • Stock levels in Excel that don’t match
  • “There must be more somewhere”
  • Stock counts that stop work
  • Wrong items shipped
  • Supplier orders by guesswork
  • Lost lots and expiry dates

Why an Odoo warehouse system

The warehouse isn’t a separate world. When it is in one system with sales and purchasing, every order reserves stock and every delivery adds to it, without anyone re-typing.

Connected to sales

A confirmed order automatically reserves the goods and creates a shipping task.

Connected to purchasing

When stock falls below the minimum, the system suggests a supplier order.

Barcode scanning

With an OCA community module, the warehouse scans with a handheld scanner or the phone camera.

Multiple warehouses and locations

Every warehouse, shop or sales van can be a separate location with visible stock.

No per-user licence

Everyone in the warehouse can use the system without increasing the monthly bill.

Traceability

Every movement stays in the history: who moved the goods, when, from where and to where.

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find an answer? Call +359 877 944 224.

Do we need special scanners?+

Not necessarily. You can work with a phone camera, but with more movement we recommend handheld barcode scanners, because they are faster and more durable.

Isn’t Odoo’s barcode module only in the paid version?+

The official scanning module is in Enterprise. We use free OCA community modules that provide scanning for receiving, picking and stock counts.

What if our items have no barcode?+

We generate internal barcodes and print labels for items and shelves.

Can it work with our online store?+

Yes. We connect the WooCommerce store to the warehouse, so stock on the website is accurate and orders go straight to shipping.

Can we track expiry dates?+

Yes. Lots can have an expiry date, and the system suggests shipping the goods with the shortest shelf life first.

Do we need a full ERP system?+

The warehouse works best together with sales and purchasing, but we can start with the warehouse alone and add the rest later.
