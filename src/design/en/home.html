Frequently asked questions

Something else? Ask us directly.

How much does a project cost?+

It depends on the scope. After the first conversation you receive a quote with a fixed price, a timeline and an exact description of what is included. If the project is for the more distant future, you can wait for a suitable EU programme. See the funding section.

How long does implementation take?+

A typical project built on a proven foundation with add-ons takes about 45 days. Custom software depends on the scope, and the timeline is written into the quote.

Is it suitable for a small company?+

Yes. We work mainly with small and medium-sized companies and start with what you need now. The system grows as your business grows.

Will it work with the software we already use?+

In most cases, yes. We connect the system to accounting and inventory software, couriers and payment services so data is never entered twice.

What happens after implementation?+

You get 24 months of warranty support and access to our portal, where you submit requests and track their status. If something goes wrong, we respond within 2 hours.

Who owns the system and the data?+

You do. Wherever possible we use open-source software with no monthly per-user licences, and the data stays on your own server.
