What business analysis is

Business analysis is the step before software. We look at how work actually flows in the company, where the problem areas are and which solution makes sense, rather than which software is fashionable right now.

Many digital projects fail not because the software is bad, but because the wrong problem was solved. Analysis reduces that risk and makes quotes comparable.

Without analysis

  • You buy software that doesn’t fit
  • The project gets more expensive along the way
  • Everyone understands the task differently
  • The wrong thing gets automated
  • Quotes can’t be compared
  • The team doesn’t accept the new system

It makes sense if

  • You are not sure what solution you need
  • You receive quotes you can’t compare
  • A previous software project didn’t work out
  • Different departments want different things

Maybe not yet, if

Frequently asked questions

Are we obliged to work with you afterwards?+

No. The plan is yours and you can use it with other vendors too.

How much of our team’s time does it take?+

A few meetings with management and short conversations with people from each department. We try not to interrupt work.

How is it different from the free consultation?+

The consultation is a conversation in which we understand the task. The analysis is an in-depth study with a written result.

Can it be used for a funding application?+

A detailed plan with requirements and a budget is a useful basis when preparing a project proposal.

Do you always recommend your own services?+

No. If the best solution is a ready-made product we don’t offer, we will recommend it.

What if it turns out we don’t need software?+

We will tell you. Sometimes the solution is a change in the process, not a new program.
