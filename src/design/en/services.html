How do I know which service I need?+

You don’t need to know in advance. Tell us what is getting in the way of your work, and at the free consultation we will suggest whether a proven system with add-ons, connecting what you already use, or software written from scratch is the better choice.

How much does a system cost?+

It depends on the scope. After the conversation you receive a quote with a specific list of features, a timeline and a fixed price, with no hidden costs.

Do we have to replace the software we already use?+

Not necessarily. It is often better value to keep it and connect it with integrations. We will tell you honestly what is worth replacing and what isn’t.

Do we pay monthly licences?+

Odoo Community, Moodle, Metabase and Nextcloud are open source and have no per-user fee. Costs after launch are for the server and, if you wish, for support.

Do you work remotely?+

Yes. Meetings, training and support can be entirely online, and when the task requires it, for example networking and backups in the office, we come on site.

Can the project be funded by the EU?+

Sometimes, yes. If your idea is for the future, we can put you on the waiting list, and when a suitable programme opens you can apply together with our partner. This takes between 1 and 2 years, and not every project can be funded.
