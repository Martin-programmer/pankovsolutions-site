How we started

Before I wrote code, I spent years working in our family restaurant: on the floor, at the till, on the busiest nights. There I saw up close how much time a small business loses re-typing, counting and searching for information, and how little of the software on the market is made for people who have no time for complicated programs.

Then I worked as a developer on large projects: systems for the Bulgarian National Bank, for the introduction of the euro, for the financial sector, for government institutions and for air traffic management. There I learned how to build systems that are not allowed to stop, and how important clear processes, documentation and security are.

Pankov Solutions brings the two together. The approach and quality of large projects, applied to small and medium-sized companies, at a price and timeline that make sense for them. No unnecessary licences, no intermediaries, and no software that only looks good in the presentation.

How we work

Six rules that apply to every project, whether it is an online store or a system for the whole company.

You talk to the person who builds it

There is no salesperson passing your request on. The person at the first meeting is the same one who sets up the system and trains your team.

First we understand, then we propose

We don’t sell a ready-made box. We start from how you work today, and only then choose a solution.

Honest, even when it doesn’t pay for us

If something isn’t worth it, we say so. Sometimes the right answer is a smaller project, or none at all.

A proven foundation, not reinventing the wheel

We build on proven platforms such as Odoo, WordPress and Moodle, and write code only where it makes sense.

A clear price and timeline before you sign

The quote contains a specific scope, timeline and fixed price. No surprises halfway through the project.

We don’t disappear after handover

Warranty, support and a request portal. The system has to keep working after we’ve gone.
