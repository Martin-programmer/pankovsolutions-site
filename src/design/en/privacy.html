What data we process and why

Enquiry form

Your name, company (optional), email, phone and a short description of what you need. We process them to reply to your enquiry and hold pre-contractual discussions, on the basis of Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps at the data subject’s request before entering into a contract). Providing them is not mandatory, but without a name, email and phone we cannot contact you.

Eligibility survey

On the EU funding pages there is a step-by-step survey: whether the company was registered before a certain date, staff and revenue ranges, company name, company ID, town, activity, what you want to fund, and finally your full name, email and phone.

The answers about the company are sent to us after each completed step, not only when you press the button at the end. This lets us see how far people get and where they stop, so we can improve the survey (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)). The data about the person (name, email, phone) is sent only when the survey is completed, so that we can contact you (Art. 6(1)(b)). The notification reaches us by email and Telegram, as with the enquiry form.

Spam protection

When a form is submitted, we process your IP address, the time of submission and technical browser signals for the anti-bot check, on the basis of legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)): to avoid automated and malicious enquiries. The IP address is kept for up to 1 hour to limit the number of enquiries from one address and is included in the notification we receive about the enquiry.

Anonymous analytics

The website measures traffic with Umami, without cookies, without a visitor identifier and without cross-site tracking. The IP address is not stored. The data is aggregated (pages, country, device type) and cannot be linked to a specific person.

Advertising and measurement (only with your consent)

We advertise on Google and Meta (Facebook and Instagram). If you accept analytics and marketing cookies in the banner, Google Analytics, the Google Ads tag and the Meta pixel are loaded on the website. They collect cookie identifiers, the pages visited, the source of the visit (for example which ad you clicked), device and browser type, IP address and whether you sent an enquiry.

We use this data to understand which ads and pages work, to measure enquiries from ads and to show our ads to people who have already visited the website. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 4a of the Bulgarian Electronic Communications Act). We do not send the content of the enquiry form to Google or Meta.

Without consent these tools are not loaded at all and set no cookies. You can withdraw your consent at any time via “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page.

Server logs

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) briefly keeps technical logs (IP address, requested address, time) for the security and operation of the service, on the basis of legitimate interest.

Source of the data

The data comes only from you. We do not make decisions by automated means that have legal or similar effects on you. If you have accepted marketing cookies, Google and Meta may include you in an advertising audience based on your visit to the website.

Retention period

  • Enquiry and correspondence: 12 months after the last communication.
  • If a contract is signed: the data becomes part of the contract documentation and is kept in accordance with accounting and tax legislation, for up to 10 years.
  • IP address for spam protection: up to 1 hour.
  • Server logs: according to Cloudflare’s policy, usually a few days.
  • Data in Google Analytics: 14 months, after which it is deleted automatically.
  • Cookies: according to the durations in the Cookie policy. Your choice in the banner is kept for 12 months, after which the banner is shown again.
