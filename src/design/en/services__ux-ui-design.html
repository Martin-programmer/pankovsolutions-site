What UX/UI design is

UX (User Experience) is how the product works: which steps a person goes through to get something done, and how easy that is. UI (User Interface) is how it looks: colours, fonts, buttons and layout.

Good design is the least noticeable. People simply get their work done quickly and without mistakes. Bad design costs money every day: in support calls, errors and abandoned orders.

Without good design

  • People ask “where do I click?”
  • Data entry errors
  • Abandoned orders and sign-ups
  • Training for every little thing
  • Every screen looks different
  • Changes that are expensive once coded

What’s the difference between UX and UI?+

UX is how the product works and how easily work gets done. UI is how it looks. The two go together.

Can we get design only, without development?+

Yes. The design and the prototype are yours and can be handed over to another team for development.

Can you improve an existing product?+

Yes. We start with a review of the current screens and user testing to see where the problem is.

Which tools do you use?+

We use modern prototyping tools that open in the browser and can be shared with a link.

How many users are needed for a test?+

Usually a handful of people uncover most of the serious problems. There’s no need for a large study.

Do you design logos and branding?+

We work with your brand. For creating a new brand from scratch, we recommend a graphic designer.
