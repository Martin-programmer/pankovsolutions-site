What business intelligence (BI) is

Business intelligence turns the data your company already collects into answers: what sells, which customer is profitable, which warehouse is overstocked, when the money comes in.

Instead of someone exporting spreadsheets at the end of the month and assembling them by hand, the dashboards update themselves and everyone sees the numbers they need.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need an ERP system?+

Not necessarily, but the data has to be in some kind of database: ERP, CRM, online store or another program. If everything is in Excel, we usually organise the data first.

Why not Power BI?+

Power BI is a strong tool, but it is paid monthly per user and tied to the Microsoft cloud. Metabase is open source, runs on your own server and is easier for people without technical experience.

Can we use data from several programs?+

Yes. Metabase connects to many kinds of databases, so one dashboard can contain data from the ERP system and from the online store.

How up to date are the numbers?+

Dashboards usually read the data directly, so they are up to date the moment you open them.

Can we create new reports ourselves?+

Yes. That is what Metabase is built for. After a short training, managers ask new questions without programming.

Is the data secure?+

Metabase runs on your own server, with login and role-based permissions. Everyone sees only the dashboards they have access to.
