What a document cloud is

A document cloud is a shared place for your company’s files, accessible from the office, from home and from a phone. Every change is synced, and everyone sees only the folders they have access to.

Instead of files sent around by email, on USB sticks and in personal cloud accounts, documents are in one place, with version history and the ability to restore them.

Today

  • Files by email and on USB sticks
  • “Which is the latest version?”
  • Personal Google accounts for company files
  • A deleted file that exists nowhere else
  • A former employee still has access
  • Files on one computer in the office

How is it better than Google Drive?+

It isn’t necessarily better for everyone. The advantage is that the files are on your own server, there is no monthly per-user fee and you have full control over who sees what.

Where are the files physically?+

On a server of your choice: in a cloud data centre in Europe or on a device in your office.

What if the server breaks?+

That is why we set up automatic off-site backups and check that recovery works.

Can we edit documents online?+

Yes. Nextcloud can be connected to an office suite for editing documents and spreadsheets in the browser.

How much space do we need?+

It depends on the volume of your files. We start with as much as you need and expand when necessary.

Do we need an IT specialist?+

No. Maintenance, updates and monitoring can be part of our support.
