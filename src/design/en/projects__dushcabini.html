The task

Dosev Impex sells bathroom furnishings from its showroom in Varna. Standard cabins and screens can be sold online like any other product, but many orders are shower screens made to exact measurements. Each of these quotes was done by hand: the customer calls and describes their bathroom, a consultant calculates the price and sends it back by phone or email. It took time on both sides, and many enquiries never turned into an order.

The goal was clear: a store where standard products are bought directly, and made-to-order screens are assembled online with a live price and reach the consultant as a ready enquiry with all the details.

A store where things are easy to find

Standard cabins, trays and heaters are bought directly. Customers filter by screen type, opening type and size without the page reloading, and every product shows its dimensions, glass, profile and related items.

The red “Custom shower screen” button is always in the menu, so it leads to the configurator from every page.

How the configurator works

Seven short steps instead of a phone call. At every step, the price and the summary on the right update instantly.

  1. Screen type

    Sliding, corner, hinged door, double sliding and more, each with a clear diagram.

  2. Dimensions

    Width, depth and height with sliders and fields, within the allowed limits.

  3. Glass type

    Clear, extra clear, frosted or smoked.

  4. Glass thickness

    Standard or thicker for more stability.

  5. Profile and fittings colour

    Every option has a photo, so it’s clear how it will look.

  6. Extra options

    Only the options that fit the chosen screen type are shown.

  7. Enquiry

    Name, phone and up to 5 photos of the bathroom. The consultant receives everything by email and in the enquiry list.

How the price is calculated

Each option has a fixed amount and a price per square metre of glass, multiplied by the area. Percentage mark-ups are added at the end. The area depends on the screen type: width by height for a door or panel, and width plus depth by height for a corner screen. The customer sees a range, for example from €560 to €700, because exact measurements are taken on site.

The biggest challenges

Hundreds of combinations and one clear price

Seven steps, dozens of options and combinations that don’t go together. We built rules that hide incompatible options and a formula that calculates the price instantly in the browser, so the customer never waits.

Letting the client manage everything themselves

The screens are Dosev’s product, and they need to be able to change prices, photos and options without a developer. So the configurator is a custom plugin with an admin panel in Bulgarian, and the whole admin panel was reworked so staff see only what they need.

No annual licences

Off-the-shelf WooCommerce configurators are paid for every year and still don’t cover the logic needed. We wrote a custom plugin licensed only for dushcabini.com, with no subscriptions for the client.

14 days for everything

A two-language store, the configurator, a guide and training had to be ready in two weeks. We built on our proven foundation of WordPress, WooCommerce and WoodMart, with translation through WPML. The store was accepted on day 13.

An admin panel made for their team

The standard WordPress panel has dozens of menus staff will never use. We made a custom home screen with tiles only for the things Dosev do every day, and a “Store manager” role that sees only those.

Everything from one screen

Orders, configurator enquiries, messages, products, customers, reports and Speedy waybills.

The website changes without a developer

The hero banner, the showroom and warehouse opening hours and a site-wide notice, each from a simple form.

Hints instead of training

Warnings for out-of-stock products and short step-by-step instructions right where they are needed.

They manage the configurator too

Every step and option is edited in one place: photo, text in both languages, fixed price, price per square metre, mark-up and rules for when to hide it.

Enquiries are kept in their own list with statistics, and the notification email is changed from the settings.
