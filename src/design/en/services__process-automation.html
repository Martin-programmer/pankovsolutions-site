What process automation is

Every company has tasks that repeat every day or every week: sending a reminder, checking which contract is expiring, compiling a report, assigning a new enquiry.

Automation takes over these tasks. The rules are set once, and the system carries them out precisely and on time, with no days off and without forgetting.

Without automation

  • Reminders that get forgotten
  • Reports assembled every Monday
  • Contracts that expire unnoticed
  • Enquiries without an owner
  • Customers who don’t come back
  • One task, five emails

With automation

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find an answer? Call +359 877 944 224.

What can be automated?+

Any task that repeats and has clear rules: reminders, emails, assignment, reports, documents and data exchange.

Do we need a new system?+

Not necessarily. Automations are often set up in the systems you already use, such as Odoo or your online store.

Can we change the rules ourselves?+

Yes. Texts, deadlines and thresholds are exposed as settings that can be changed without a developer.

What if an automation goes wrong?+

Every run is recorded in a log, and if there’s an error, the responsible person gets a notification.

How much time does it save?+

It depends on the process. Before we start, we estimate how long the task takes today, so the benefit is clear.

Are emails sent from our address?+

Yes. We set up sending from your domain so emails reach customers instead of their spam folder.
