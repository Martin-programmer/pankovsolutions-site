What a CRM system is

A CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system is where a company keeps everything about its customers: who they are, what they asked, what you offered them, when you should call them and what they bought.

Instead of each salesperson keeping their customers on their phone and in their personal inbox, the information is shared. When someone is on holiday or leaves, the customers and their history stay with the company.

Today

  • Enquiries in email, Viber and notebooks
  • Nobody knows who called whom
  • Forgotten calls and quotes
  • Customers leave with the salesperson
  • Management can’t see what’s coming
  • Sales reports made by hand

How it works in practice

The path of an enquiry from first contact to a signed contract. Every step is recorded on the customer.

  1. EnquiryComes from the website, email or phone.
  2. AssignmentGoes to the right salesperson.
  3. MeetingThe conversation and notes are recorded.
  4. QuoteSent from the system.
  5. DecisionA reminder if the customer goes quiet.
  6. DealThe customer is won; the history stays.

How much does a CRM system cost?+

It depends on the number of stages, fields, automations and the data to migrate. After the free consultation you receive a quote with a fixed price. There is no monthly per-user fee, as Odoo Community is open source.

How is it better than HubSpot or Pipedrive?+

They are convenient, but you pay every month for every user and the data is in someone else’s system. Odoo has no per-user licence, runs on your own server and can grow into a full ERP system.

Can it connect to our website?+

Yes. Enquiry forms on the website create new enquiries directly in the CRM, with information about which page they came from.

Will our salespeople use it?+

That’s why we start from how they work and remove everything unnecessary. The fewer fields to fill in, the more likely the system is to be used.

Does it work on a phone?+

Yes. The CRM opens in the phone’s browser, so salespeople record notes and tasks right after the meeting.

Can we add quotes and invoicing later?+

Yes. That is one of Odoo’s biggest advantages. Sales, inventory and invoicing are added as modules in the same system.
