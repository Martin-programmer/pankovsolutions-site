Why backups matter

A broken disk, a stolen laptop, a deleted folder or an attack that encrypts your files. The question is not whether it will happen, but when. Then the only thing that matters is whether there is a copy and whether it can be restored.

Many companies think they have backups until they try to use them. That’s why we don’t just set up backups; we also test recovery.

Typical problems

  • No backups at all
  • The backup is on the same disk
  • Copies are made by hand when someone remembers
  • Nobody has tried to restore
  • An attack encrypts the backups too
  • Laptops are not backed up

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find an answer? Call +359 877 944 224.

How often are backups made?+

Usually every night, and more often for important systems. The schedule depends on how much data you can afford to lose.

Where are the backups kept?+

On a device in the office for fast recovery, and in a second location outside the office for protection against a serious event.

Are they protected from ransomware?+

We configure the backups so that an attack on the computers can’t delete or change them.

How fast is recovery?+

It depends on the volume. A single file comes back in minutes, a whole server usually in hours.

How much space is needed?+

Backups store only the changes, so the space needed is much smaller than the size of all versions.

How do we know it works?+

You get a notification if there’s a problem, and we periodically run a test restore.
