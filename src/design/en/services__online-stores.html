Frequently asked questions

How much does an online store cost?+

It depends on the number of products, the design, the integrations and special features such as configurators. After the consultation you receive a quote with a fixed price. There is no percentage of sales.

Can you migrate our store from another platform?+

Yes. We have migrated a store from CloudCart to WooCommerce, together with its products, customers and orders.

Which couriers can it work with?+

We connect the store to the courier companies you work with, so the shipping cost is calculated and the waybill is created from the admin panel.

What is needed for card payments?+

A contract for a virtual POS terminal with a bank or with a service such as Stripe. We help with the technical side, including integration with BORICA.

Does it meet the requirements in Bulgaria?+

We discuss with you and your accountant how sales are to be reported and configure the store accordingly. We also prepare the terms and conditions, the right of withdrawal and the cookie consent.

Can we add products ourselves?+

Yes. We train your team and leave a guide, so products, prices and promotions are managed without us.
