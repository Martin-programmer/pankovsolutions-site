What a learning platform is

A learning platform (LMS, Learning Management System) is where a company’s courses live: video, documents, presentations and quizzes, together with information about who has completed what.

A new employee doesn’t wait for a colleague to have time to explain. They log in, work through the courses at their own pace, take a quiz and get a certificate, and management sees their progress.

Today

  • Training “on the fly” from colleagues
  • Every new person takes weeks
  • Knowledge lives in experienced people’s heads
  • No check of what has been understood
  • Documents scattered across folders
  • Nobody knows who has completed what

You need it if

  • You hire people regularly
  • Training depends on one or two colleagues
  • New people make the same mistakes
  • You need to prove qualifications
  • You also want to train customers or partners

Maybe not yet, if

  • You have two or three employees who don’t change
  • Training is entirely hands-on, on site

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find an answer? Call +359 877 944 224.

Who creates the course content?+

The content is yours, because you know the work. We help you structure it, upload your existing materials and prepare the quizzes.

Can it be used on a phone?+

Yes. The platform works in the phone’s browser, and there is also a free Moodle mobile app.

Can we train customers too?+

Yes. There can be separate courses and groups for employees, customers and partners, each with their own access.

How many learners can there be?+

There is no per-learner licence. The only limit is the capacity of the server, which is chosen based on the number of people.

Can we upload ready-made courses?+

Yes. Moodle supports SCORM packages created with tools such as Articulate or iSpring.

What happens to learners’ personal data?+

The platform runs on your own server, and the data is not shared with third parties.
