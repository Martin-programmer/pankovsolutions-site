WordPress or React – which is better for us?+

If you will update the content often and yourselves, WordPress. If the site changes rarely and you want maximum speed, React. We will recommend one after our conversation.

Do you write the copy?+

We help with the structure and messages and edit your texts. But the best source of information about your company is you.

Can the website be in several languages?+

Yes. We have built websites in Bulgarian, English and German, with separate versions for each language.

Will it be on Google?+

We get all the technical basics right for search engines: speed, structure, titles and descriptions. Rankings also depend on content and competition, so we don’t promise first place.

Is hosting included?+

We can take care of hosting, the domain and the SSL certificate as part of support, or upload the site to your own hosting.

Can we add a store later?+

Yes. A WordPress website can be extended with WooCommerce when you decide to sell online.
