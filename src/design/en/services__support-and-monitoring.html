What support is

After launch, the system begins its real life. Questions come up, new employees join, changes are needed, updates arrive and sometimes there are problems. Support is how all of this happens quickly and in an orderly way.

It includes monitoring, updates, answering questions, fixes and small improvements. Requests are submitted through a portal, so you can see who is working on them and when they will be done.

Without support

  • Problems get solved when someone has time
  • Requests lost in emails and calls
  • Systems that aren’t updated
  • Nobody checks that everything works
  • Every small change is a new project
  • Nobody knows who is responsible

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find an answer? Call +359 877 944 224.

What does a response within 2 hours mean?+

During working hours, within 2 hours of a problem being reported, we confirm we are taking it on and start working on it.

What does the warranty cover?+

Defects in what we delivered are fixed at no extra charge for 24 months after acceptance.

Do you support systems built by others?+

It’s possible after a review of the system. First we assess its condition.

How do I submit a request?+

Through the support portal, where every request has a number, a status and a history. In urgent cases, also by phone.

Are changes included?+

Small changes can be part of the package. Larger ones are estimated separately before we start.

Is support mandatory after the warranty?+

No. You can choose a package, pay per request, or take over the system yourselves.
