When custom software makes sense

Ready-made systems cover general needs. When your process is specific, when you want to give customers something unique or you are building a new product, the off-the-shelf solution starts getting in the way.

That’s when we write an application from scratch, exactly for your case. It runs in the browser, can be added to the phone’s home screen like a real app and is managed from its own admin panel.

Frequently asked questions

Can’t find an answer? Call +359 877 944 224.

How much does a custom application cost?+

It depends entirely on the features. That’s why we start with analysis and a prototype, after which you receive a quote with a specific scope, timeline and fixed price.

What is an MVP?+

The minimum version of the product that solves the main problem and can be tried by real users. It shows whether the idea works before you invest in the full version.

Does it need to be in the App Store and Google Play?+

Not necessarily. A progressive web app is added to the home screen and works like a real one. If needed, we can also package it for the stores.

Who owns the code?+

Ownership and licensing of the code are agreed in the contract. We discuss them openly before starting.

What happens after launch?+

We offer support with a warranty, monitoring and development of new features.

Can you continue an application written by someone else?+

Yes, after reviewing the code. Sometimes it is better to continue it, sometimes to rewrite it. We will tell you honestly.
